05 / FAQ

GOT QUESTIONS?

What types of Web3 projects do you work with?

We work with Meme Coins, DeFi protocols, Web3 ecosystems, NFT collections, DAOs and independent crypto projects.

Can you build a website from scratch?

Yes. We can help with visual direction, page structure, responsive development and deployment.

Can you help connect a custom domain?

Yes. We can assist with domain configuration, DNS records and HTTPS setup. Domain registration costs are separate.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the page count, content and functionality. We will provide an estimated timeline after understanding your requirements.

How do I start a project?

Send us your project introduction, preferred style and website requirements through Telegram or email. We will discuss the next steps.
